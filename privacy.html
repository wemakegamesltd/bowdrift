BOWDRIFT

Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version: Bowdrift collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no tracking and no advertising. Your scores and progress stay on your own phone, and we never see them.

01

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the mobile game Bowdrift: Arrows & Balloons, published by We Make Games Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17393439), registered office 124-128 City Road, London, England, EC1V 2NX.

You can reach us at wemakegamesltd@protonmail.com.

02

What we collect

Nothing. Bowdrift has no sign-in, no user accounts and no way for you to send us information. We do not use analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting or any other tool that gathers data about you or your device.

We do not know who you are, how often you play, or how well you score.

03

What the game stores on your phone

So that your progress is still there when you come back, the game saves the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your settings: sound, music and music volume, vibration, aiming and score lines
  • your best round, best score and most balloons popped
  • the points you have banked, and the bows, finishes, arrows and trails you have unlocked
  • how far your Chill mode journey has reached
  • how much of today's free play you have used, and the day you first played
  • whether you have bought unlimited play
  • the score of a Challenge game that was interrupted, so its points are not lost

This information never leaves your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes all of it.

04

Payments

Bowdrift is free to play for a set time each day. Unlimited play is a one-off purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Your payment goes to Google, not to us. We never see or handle your card details, billing address or any other payment information.

When the game starts, and when you buy or tap Already paid? Restore, the game asks Google Play whether the Google account on your device owns unlimited play, and Google answers yes or no. That answer is used only to open up unlimited play on your device. It is not stored by us and not sent anywhere else.

Google's handling of your purchase is covered by Google's own privacy policy. If you need a refund, or have a question about a payment, Google is the right place to start — though you are welcome to contact us too.

05

Internet access

Bowdrift plays entirely offline. Everything in the game, including its music, is inside the app itself.

The app is able to use the internet, and does so only to reach Google Play about the purchase described above.

06

Vibration

The game asks permission to use your phone's vibration, for a small buzz when you shoot and when a balloon pops. You can switch this off at any time in Options.

07

Children

Bowdrift collects no data from anyone, of any age, so it collects no data from children either.

08

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data held about you, including the right to see it, correct it and have it deleted. Because we hold no personal data about you at all, there is nothing for us to show you or delete — but do get in touch if you would like that confirmed in writing.

For anything Google holds in connection with a purchase, contact Google directly. If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

09

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Any version of the game already installed on your phone continues to behave exactly as it did when you installed it.
